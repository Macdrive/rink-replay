Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to rink.replay@gmail.com, or choose Help ▸ Submit Feedback… in the app, which can attach the app's logs (never your videos).

The app's own help (Help ▸ Rink Replay Help, ⌘?) explains every feature step by step.

Getting started

How should I record? For two cameras, put one behind or beside each net, both filming the whole game. For one camera, follow a player. Keep each camera's videos in its own folder; the files can stay where they are.

How do I start? Choose File ▸ New Project…, pick one or two cameras, drop the folders and, if you like, type the team names. The project keeps your work and saves as you go.

Why does the first opening take a while? The app analyses the footage once (syncing the cameras, finding the players) and keeps the result. With two cameras it then looks for the puck in the whole game, about 12 minutes for a game in 1080p; you can watch and edit meanwhile.

What is the rink map? On a frame from each camera you draw the ice, the lines and the nets once per rink. With it the app finds the game start, the goals and the puck more reliably.

Exporting and Unlimited

What's free? Everything but exporting is free for every game. The first two games you export are free, and you can export them again as often as you like.

What does Rink Replay Unlimited do? It's a one-time purchase that unlocks exporting for every game: the whole game video and event clips. It's shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought Unlimited on another Mac or reinstalled the app. Open Rink Replay ▸ Settings… and click Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Troubleshooting

The app asks me to locate a camera folder. macOS lets the app open only folders you chose. If a project's videos moved, or the project was made with an older version, choose the folder once and the app remembers it.

The wrong camera is chosen at a moment. Click that stretch of the coloured camera bar on the timeline to flip it to the other camera; drawing the rink map improves the automatic choice.

A goal is missing or wrong. Right-click the timeline to add a goal, or remove a suggested one; fix a wrong scoring team with ✎ in the goal's row.
