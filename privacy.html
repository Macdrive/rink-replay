Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Your videos stay on your Mac. Rink Replay analyses and edits them on the Mac itself; videos, pictures from them, file names, folders and team names are never sent anywhere.

What the app sends

To find and fix problems, the app sends two kinds of reports through Google Firebase. Both can be turned off at any time in Rink Replay ▸ Settings… ▸ Privacy; the change applies at once.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the app crashes or a task such as a render fails, a report with the error, where in the app it happened, the Mac's model, the macOS and app versions, and the app's last log lines with names and paths removed.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): which features are used (such as opening a project, analysing, rendering, exporting), whether they worked, and the number of cameras in a project. Google Analytics also records basic app information such as the app version, the macOS version, sessions and an approximate region (for example the country) inferred from the network connection.

Both use a random identifier created for the app installation. They are not linked to your name, email or Apple Account, are not used for advertising and are not combined with data from other apps or websites.

The app also downloads settings from Firebase Remote Config (such as a message on the start screen); this sends nothing about you beyond what's needed to fetch them.

Google processes this data on our behalf; see Firebase privacy and security.

Purchases

Rink Replay Unlimited is bought through the App Store; Apple handles the payment and we receive no payment details. If usage statistics are on, Google Analytics records that the purchase was made (the product, its name and price), with the same random identifier and nothing that identifies you. To count the two free games, the app keeps a list of random project identifiers in your Mac's keychain. It never leaves your Mac.

Feedback

When you choose Help ▸ Submit Feedback…, the app prepares an email that you check and send yourself. With Include logs and performance data, it attaches the app's log of the last days (it names video files, not their folders) and timing data. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Children

Rink Replay is a video tool for parents, players and coaches and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children; footage of players stays on your Mac.

Your choices and rights

Turn off crash reports and usage statistics in Settings ▸ Privacy. To ask about, or delete, data sent from your Mac, write to us; because reports aren't linked to you, include the approximate date so we can find them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
