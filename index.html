Two cameras. One game video.

Rink Replay turns your hockey recordings into a game video and short clips of the moments that matter, on your Mac.

What it does

  • Edits the game for you. With a camera at each end of the rink, it syncs them by sound and switches to the camera where the play is, following the players and the puck.
  • Leaves out the breaks. Warm-up, intermissions and stoppages are cut; you can adjust the start, end and cuts on the timeline.
  • Finds the goals. It suggests goals and which team scored; you confirm them and add shots, saves and penalties.
  • Makes clips to share. Export the events as separate clips or one video, with the score bar or event names and slow-motion replays.
  • One camera works too. Follow a player with one camera and mark the moments yourself.
Free to try. Analysing, watching and marking events are free for every game, and currently your first three games export free, as often as you like. Rink Replay Unlimited, a subscription (monthly, for a six-month season, or yearly), unlocks exporting for every game and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
  • Game recordings from one or two action cameras (for example DJI Osmo Action), each camera's videos in its own folder.

Your videos never leave your Mac: all analysis and editing happen on it.
